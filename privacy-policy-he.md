# Privacy Policy - Tehillim

Last updated: November 2026

## Overview

This application is a Hebrew Tehillim (Psalms) and prayer application that works fully offline.

The app runs locally on your device, without requiring an account, an internet connection, or personal information.

## Data Collection

The developer does not collect, receive, or share your personal data.

Specifically:

- No account creation
- No login system
- No analytics
- No tracking
- No advertising identifiers
- No location collection
- No contact access
- No microphone or camera access
- No cloud synchronization by the app

## Information Stored on the Device

The app stores the following information on your device:

- Favorite chapters
- Reading progress and the last reading position
- The order and visibility of the prayer list
- Settings and display preferences, such as light or dark mode, text size, prayer rite, and selected city
- Reminder settings, including any reminder text you write
- Names recently used to read Psalm 119

The app does not send this information to the developer.

## Device Backup and Transfer

If device backup is turned on, or if you move your data to a new device, the operating system may copy the app's information through Apple's or Google's services or directly between the devices. The developer has no access to these copies. You can manage backup in your device's settings.

The names recently used to read Psalm 119 are the exception: starting with version 1.47, the app excludes them from backup and transfer. On iPhone, backups made by earlier versions may still contain them.

## Notifications

If you choose to turn on reminders, they are scheduled on the device itself using the device's notification system. You can turn them off at any time in the app's settings or in the device's settings.

## Internet Usage

The app works fully offline.

External links, such as rating or sharing the app, open outside the app, in the app store or in the device's browser.

Review requests, and update checks on Android, are carried out by Google Play or the App Store under their own privacy policies.

## Text Sources

The app contains the Book of Psalms and traditional Hebrew prayers, derived from classical liturgical sources recognized as being in the public domain.

The texts in the app are provided for prayer and study, in a format that works offline.

The app itself, including its design, interface, structure, and implementation, was developed independently and is not officially affiliated with any religious institution or publisher.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Security

Because the developer does not collect user data, there is no user account and no personal information stored on the developer's servers.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be published on this page.

## Contact

Choosing to contact us in the app opens the mail app. Information is sent only if you choose to send a message yourself.

For questions regarding this privacy policy, you can reach us at:

psalms.app10@gmail.com
