# Privacy Policy - Tehillim

Last updated: September 2026

## Overview

This application is a Hebrew Tehillim (Psalms) and prayer application that works fully offline.

The app runs locally on your device, without requiring an account, an internet connection, or personal information.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.

Specifically:

- No account creation
- No login system
- No analytics
- No tracking
- No advertising identifiers
- No location collection
- No contact access
- No microphone or camera access
- No cloud synchronization

## Information Stored on the Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

- Favorite chapters
- Reading progress
- Settings and display preferences, such as light or dark mode, text size, and prayer rite
- Reminder settings

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

## Notifications

If you choose to turn on reminders, they are scheduled on the device itself using the device's notification system. You can turn them off at any time in the app's settings or in the device's settings.

## Internet Usage

The app works fully offline.

External links, such as rating or sharing the app, open outside the app, in the app store or in the device's browser.

## Text Sources

The app contains the Book of Psalms and traditional Hebrew prayers, derived from classical liturgical sources recognized as being in the public domain.

The texts in the app are provided for prayer and study, in a format that works offline.

The app itself, including its design, interface, structure, and implementation, was developed independently and is not officially affiliated with any religious institution or publisher.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Security

Because the app does not collect or transmit user data, there is no user account and no personal information stored on external servers.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version will be published on this page.

## Contact

Choosing to contact us in the app opens the mail app. Information is sent only if you choose to send a message yourself.

For questions regarding this privacy policy, you can reach us at:

psalms.app10@gmail.com
